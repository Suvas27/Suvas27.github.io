About Me

My interest in VLSI began with a curiosity about how billions of transistors work together to power the electronic devices we use every day. Exploring digital electronics, FPGA design, and hardware development showed me that chip design blends creativity, engineering, and problem-solving in a unique way.

What drives me further is the pace of change in the semiconductor industry — AI, high-performance computing, automotive electronics, and IoT are pushing demand for smarter, more efficient chips, and India's growing role as a global semiconductor hub makes this an exciting time to be entering the field.

I believe continuous learning and attention to detail are what separate a good VLSI engineer from a great one — and that's the standard I'm building toward.
